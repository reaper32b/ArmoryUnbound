Armory Unbound - your own skins

Drop .png or .jpg images in this folder and restart the game. Each image becomes a skin
(id "auSkin" + the file name, letters and digits only: tiger_stripe.png -> auSkinTigerStripe).
Square, seamless (tileable) images look best; the pattern is tiled about 4 times across each gun texture.
Images stay on your PC; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
